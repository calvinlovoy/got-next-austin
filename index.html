    About the data

    Indoor times are the free open play hours each City of Austin recreation center posted on its gym schedule, collected November 11, 2026. Outdoor courts are first come, first served during city park hours, 5 a.m. to 10 p.m.

    Distances are straight lines from the middle of the neighborhood you pick. Pages like this one can't read your device location, so choose the closest neighborhood.

    The city changes gym schedules without notice. Cards marked as possibly outdated are worth a phone call first.
