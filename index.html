    About

    Indoor times are the free open play hours each City of Austin recreation center posted on its gym schedule, collected November 11, 2026. Outdoor courts and fields come from the city's parks facility inventory and are first come, first served unless marked. Most follow city park hours, 5 a.m. to 10 p.m., and some courts post shorter hours. The city changes gym schedules without notice.
